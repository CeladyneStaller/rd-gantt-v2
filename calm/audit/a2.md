buildGraphElements, toRenderElements, buildWaterfallModel, the 91 in-browser tests). The test drives the built
boards like a person (`out/a2/Main.dc.html`, `out/a2/Sales-A.dc.html`) and checks the screen, the stored data
and the numbers. The builders' own tests were not used as evidence.
